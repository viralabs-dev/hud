PDCurses (https://pdcurses.org/, https://github.com/wmcbrine/PDCurses)
Embutido no binário do Windows, ligado estaticamente nas extensões _curses e
_curses_panel do windows-curses (que usa a cópia do PDCurses em
https://github.com/zephyrproject-rtos/PDCurses, porte "wincon" para o console
do Windows).

Situação legal, conforme o README do PDCurses ("Legal Stuff"):

  The core package and most ports are in the public domain, but a few files
  in the demos and X11 directories are subject to copyright under licenses
  described there.

  This software is provided AS IS with NO WARRANTY whatsoever.

O binário do HUD só usa o núcleo (pdcurses/) e o porte wincon/, que estão em
domínio público; nem os demos nem o porte X11 são incluídos.
